Odin the All-Father.

Odin is the chief god of Norse mythology, presiding over Asgard as the ruler of the Aesir pantheon. Often called the Allfather, he is a complex deity of wisdom, war, poetry, and death, renowned for his endless quest for knowledge. To gain ultimate understanding, he famously sacrificed one of his eyes at Mimir's well and hung himself from the world tree, Yggdrasil. He is famously accompanied by his two ravens, Huginn and Muninn, and his eight-legged steed, Sleipnir.
