Odin the All-Father.

Odin is the chief god of Norse mythology, presiding over Asgard as the ruler of the Aesir pantheon. Often called the Allfather, he is a complex deity of wisdom, war, poetry, and death, renowned for his endless quest for knowledge. To gain ultimate understanding, he famously sacrificed one of his eyes at Mimir's well and hung himself from the world tree, Yggdrasil. He is famously accompanied by his two ravens, Huginn and Muninn, and his eight-legged steed, Sleipnir.

Thor vs. Kratos

Thor wields Mjolnir, the fearsome, short-handled hammer of Thor, forged by dwarves and capable of leveling mountains with a single strike. As a symbol of divine protection, it always returns to its master's hand after being thrown and channels the raw fury of lightning and thunder.

Thor is a towering mountain of a man with a wild red mane and a heavy, beer-soaked frame, this incarnation moves like a brewing storm of raw, unpredictable violence. He is a terrifying enforcer carrying the weight of self-destruction, and a blood-stained hammer.

Kratos is a Greek demigod who, after destroying his homeland's pantheon out of vengeance, exiled himself to the Norse realm to raise his son, Atreus. He fights using the Leviathan Axe and Blades of Chaos to protect his family, ultimately choosing to become a peaceful god of restoration rather than a weapon of war.

Kratos wields the ice-infused Leviathan Axe and his classic fire-wielding Blades of Chaos, switching between freezing and burning his enemies in combat. He later adds the Draupnir Spear to his arsenal, a self-replicating weapon capable of stored kinetic detonations.
